# Replacing the cooling fan

The cooling fan of the VX-200 is a wearing part. Replace it every 3 years, or sooner if it becomes noisy or stiff, or if fault `E-16` appears.

## Fans per frame size

| Frame | Power range | Number of fans |
|---|---|---|
| A | 0.75–2.2 kW | 1 |
| B | 4–7.5 kW | 2 |

On frame B, replace both fans at the same time. Use only the replacement fan specified for the frame size.

## Preparation

1. Stop the motor.
2. Isolate the supply and lock it off.
3. Wait 5 minutes for the DC bus to discharge.
4. Let the heatsink cool.

## Removing the old fan

1. The fan module is at the bottom of the drive. Press the two clips on its sides inward.
2. Pull the fan module down and out.
3. Unplug the fan connector. Pull on the connector body, not on the wires.

## Fitting the new fan

1. Check the arrow on the new fan. It must point up into the drive, so that air is blown from the bottom to the top.
2. Plug in the connector. It fits one way only.
3. Tuck the wires clear of the blades.
4. Push the fan module up until both clips click.
5. Turn the blades by hand to check that they move freely.

## Testing

1. Restore the supply.
2. Start the drive. The fan runs when the drive runs.
3. Check that air leaves the top of the drive and that `E-16` does not appear.

Record the date of the replacement in the maintenance log.
