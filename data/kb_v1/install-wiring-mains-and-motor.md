# Wiring the mains supply and motor terminals

Carry out all wiring with the supply isolated and locked off. If the drive has been powered, wait 5 minutes for the DC bus to discharge first.

## Power terminals

| Terminal | Connection |
|---|---|
| L1, L2, L3 | 3-phase mains supply, 380–480 V AC ±10 %, 50/60 Hz |
| U, V, W | 3-phase induction motor |
| PE | Protective earth (one for the supply, one for the motor) |
| DC+, DC− | DC bus measuring points; do not connect anything |

Never connect the mains supply to U, V, W. This destroys the drive.

## Supply side

- Fit fuses or a circuit breaker upstream of each drive. Choose the rating and the cable size from the drive rating label and local wiring regulations.
- Fit an isolator that can be locked in the off position.

## Motor side

- Use a screened motor cable. The maximum motor cable length is 50 m.
- Check that the motor terminal box is linked in star or delta to suit the supply voltage, as shown on the motor nameplate.
- Do not fit a switch or contactor between the drive and the motor that operates while the drive is running.
- If the motor turns the wrong way, isolate the drive, wait 5 minutes and swap any two of U, V, W.

## Tightening torque

| Frame | Power terminals |
|---|---|
| A (0.75–2.2 kW) | 1.2 Nm |
| B (4–7.5 kW) | 2.5 Nm |

Refit the terminal cover before applying power. See the earthing article for the PE and screen connections.
