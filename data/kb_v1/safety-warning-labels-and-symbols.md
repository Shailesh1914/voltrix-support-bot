# Meaning of the warning labels and symbols on the VX-200

The VX-200 carries four warning symbols. Do not remove or cover them. If a label becomes unreadable, contact Voltrix Drives support.

## Lightning flash in a triangle: dangerous voltage

**Where:** on the front of the terminal cover.

**Meaning:** the terminals behind the cover carry dangerous voltage whenever the supply is connected, even when the motor is stopped. Isolate the supply before removing the cover.

## Lightning flash with "5 min": stored charge

**Where:** on the front of the terminal cover, next to the dangerous voltage symbol.

**Meaning:** the DC bus capacitors stay charged after the supply is switched off. Wait 5 minutes after isolating the supply before touching any terminal, then confirm with a voltmeter that the voltage between DC+ and DC− is below 50 V DC.

## Wavy lines in a triangle: hot surface

**Where:** on the side of the drive, near the heatsink.

**Meaning:** the heatsink becomes hot in operation and can reach 90 °C. Let it cool before cleaning the drive or replacing the fan.

## Open book: read the instructions

**Where:** on the rating label.

**Meaning:** read the quick-start guide and the relevant knowledge base articles before installing, commissioning or maintaining the drive.

## Earth symbol

The PE terminals are marked with the protective earth symbol, a vertical line above three horizontal lines in a circle. This is a marking, not a warning: it shows where the earth conductors must be connected. The drive must never be operated without its earth connection.

## The rating label

The rating label on the right-hand side of the drive shows the power rating, frame size, supply voltage and output current. Use it to select fuses and cables.
