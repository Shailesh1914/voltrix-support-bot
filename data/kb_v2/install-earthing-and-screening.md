# Earthing and cable screening

Correct earthing protects people from electric shock and keeps electrical interference from the drive under control. Poor earthing is a common cause of unstable analog signals and of faults such as `E-12` and `E-15`.

## Protective earth

- Connect the supply earth conductor to one PE terminal of the VX-200 and the motor earth conductor to the other.
- The earth conductor must be at least the same size as the phase conductors.
- Connect each drive to the cabinet earth bar with its own conductor. Do not loop the earth from one drive to the next.
- Tighten the PE terminals to the same torque as the power terminals: 1.2 Nm on frame A, 2.5 Nm on frame B.
- Mount the drive on a bare metal back plate. Remove paint under the fixing points so the housing makes good contact.

## Motor cable screen

- Use a screened motor cable, no longer than 50 m.
- Connect the screen to earth at both ends: at the drive and at the motor terminal box.
- Clamp the screen all the way round with a metal clamp. Do not twist the screen into a long tail.
- Keep the unscreened ends of the conductors as short as possible.

## Control cable screen

- Use screened cable for the analog inputs AI1, AI2 and the analog output AO1.
- Connect the control cable screen to earth at the drive end only.

## Cable routing

Keep motor cables, mains cables and control cables apart. Leave at least 200 mm between control cables and power cables, and cross them at a right angle where they must cross.

## Checking

Before first power-up, measure continuity between the motor frame, the drive PE terminal and the cabinet earth bar.
