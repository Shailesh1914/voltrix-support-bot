# Inspecting and re-tightening power terminals

Terminal screws can loosen over time through heating, cooling and vibration. A loose power terminal gets hot and can cause faults such as `E-03`, `E-06`, `E-10` and `E-11`. Inspect and re-tighten the terminals every 12 months.

## Preparation

1. Stop the motor.
2. Isolate the supply and lock it off.
3. Wait 5 minutes for the DC bus to discharge.
4. Remove the terminal cover.
5. Confirm with a suitable voltmeter that there is no voltage on L1, L2, L3 and less than 50 V DC between DC+ and DC−.

## Inspection

Look for:

- Discoloured or melted insulation near a terminal.
- Darkened or pitted terminal screws.
- Loose wire strands.
- Damage to the cable where it enters the cabinet.

A discoloured terminal has been running hot. Cut the conductor back to clean copper and reconnect it. If the terminal block itself is damaged, contact Voltrix Drives support.

## Re-tightening

Use a torque screwdriver.

| Terminals | Frame A (0.75–2.2 kW) | Frame B (4–7.5 kW) |
|---|---|---|
| L1, L2, L3, U, V, W, PE | 1.2 Nm | 2.5 Nm |
| Control terminals | 0.5 Nm | 0.5 Nm |

Do not exceed these values. Overtightening damages the terminal.

## Also check

- The connections in the motor terminal box.
- The upstream fuses, circuit breaker and isolator.
- The earth connection at the cabinet earth bar.

## Finishing

Refit the terminal cover, restore the supply and confirm the drive powers up without a fault. Record the inspection in the maintenance log.
