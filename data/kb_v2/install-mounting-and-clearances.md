# Mounting the drive and cabinet clearances

## Where to mount the drive

The VX-200 has an IP20 enclosure. It must be mounted inside a closed electrical cabinet that protects it from dust, moisture and accidental contact. It is not suitable for mounting in the open.

## Orientation

Mount the drive vertically on a flat, solid metal back plate, with the keypad facing forward and the fan at the bottom. Cooling air is drawn in at the bottom and leaves at the top. Do not mount the drive on its side, flat on its back or upside down.

## Clearances

Leave free space around each drive so the cooling air can flow:

| Position | Minimum clearance |
|---|---|
| Above the drive | 100 mm |
| Below the drive | 100 mm |
| Each side | 50 mm |

Do not place cable trunking or other equipment inside these spaces. When drives are mounted one above another, make sure the warm air from the lower drive is not drawn straight into the upper one.

## Air temperature

The drive gives its full rating when the air at its inlet is between −10 °C and +40 °C. Between 40 °C and 50 °C the output current must be reduced by 2.5 % for each degree above 40 °C. Do not operate the drive above 50 °C.

Measure the temperature inside the cabinet, not in the room. Fit cabinet ventilation if needed.

## Things to avoid

- Heat sources directly below the drive.
- Drilling above the drive after it is fitted; metal swarf can fall into the vents.
- Vibration, corrosive gases and conductive dust.

Poor cooling leads to fault `E-07`.
