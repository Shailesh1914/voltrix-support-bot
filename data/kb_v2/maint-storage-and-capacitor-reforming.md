# Storing the drive and re-forming capacitors after long storage

## Storage conditions

Store a VX-200 that is not in use as follows:

- In its original packaging, or covered against dust.
- In a clean, dry place free from condensation and corrosive gases.
- At a temperature between −20 °C and +60 °C.
- Not stacked under heavy items.

Note the date the drive went into storage on the packaging.

## Why long storage matters

The DC bus capacitors inside the drive slowly lose their internal insulating layer when they are left without voltage. If full load is applied straight after long storage, the capacitors can be damaged. Re-forming restores the layer by applying voltage with no load for a period.

## What to do after storage

| Time without power | Action before use |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 year | None. Install and commission as normal. |
| 1 to 2 years | Re-form: apply the supply for at least 1 hour with no run command. |
| More than 2 years | Do not apply power. Contact Voltrix Drives support. |

## Re-forming procedure (1 to 2 years)

1. Install and wire the drive as normal, or connect only the supply to L1, L2, L3 and PE.
2. Make sure no run command is present.
3. Switch on the supply.
4. Leave the drive powered for at least 1 hour without starting the motor.
5. The drive is then ready for normal commissioning.

## Drives kept as spares

To avoid re-forming, power a spare drive for 1 hour once a year and record the date.

## After storage in a cold place

Let the drive reach room temperature before applying power, so that no condensation remains inside.
