# Unpacking and checking the VX-200 on delivery

## Before opening the box

Check the outside of the packaging for crushing, holes or water marks. Note any damage before you open the box.

## What should be in the box

- One VX-200 drive with its terminal cover fitted.
- A printed quick-start guide.
- A factory-fitted wire link between terminals DI3 and +24V.

## Checking the rating label

The rating label is on the right-hand side of the drive. Confirm that it matches the motor and the supply:

- **Power rating:** one of 0.75, 1.5, 2.2, 4, 5.5 or 7.5 kW.
- **Frame size:** A for 0.75–2.2 kW, B for 4–7.5 kW.
- **Supply:** 3-phase 380–480 V AC, 50/60 Hz.
- **Output:** 3-phase, 0–400 Hz.

The drive rated current on the label should be equal to or higher than the rated current on the motor nameplate.

## Inspecting the drive

1. Check the housing, keypad and display for cracks.
2. Check that the terminal cover fits and that no terminal screws are missing.
3. Look through the vents for loose parts or packing material.
4. Turn the cooling fan gently by hand. It should spin freely.

## If something is wrong

Do not install or power a drive that is damaged or does not match the rating you expected. Keep the packaging and contact Voltrix Drives support with the details from the rating label and a description of the damage.

## If the drive is not installed straight away

Keep it in its packaging in a clean, dry place. See the storage article for the conditions and for what to do after long storage.
