# Routine maintenance schedule for the VX-200

The VX-200 needs little maintenance, but regular checks prevent most overheating and connection faults. Shorten the intervals below in dusty, hot or humid locations.

## Schedule

| Interval | Task | Supply |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | Visual check: display normal, no fault codes, fan running, no unusual noise or smell | On |
| Monthly | Check the cabinet temperature and the cabinet filters | On |
| Every 6 months | Clean the heatsink and air vents | Isolated |
| Every 12 months | Inspect and re-tighten the power and control terminals | Isolated |
| Every 12 months | Inspect cables for damage and discolouration | Isolated |
| Every 12 months | Read and record the fault history in `P-55` to `P-59` | On |
| Every 3 years | Replace the cooling fan | Isolated |

## Before any task with the supply isolated

1. Stop the motor.
2. Isolate the supply and lock it off.
3. Wait 5 minutes for the DC bus to discharge.
4. Confirm there is no voltage before touching any terminal.

See the safe isolation article for the full procedure.

## Records

Keep a log for each drive with the date, the task carried out, the fault history and a record of the parameter settings. A record of the settings makes it quick to re-enter them after a restore of factory defaults.

## When to contact support

Contact Voltrix Drives support if an inspection finds burnt or discoloured parts, a cracked housing, or damage inside the drive. Do not open the drive beyond the terminal cover and the fan.
